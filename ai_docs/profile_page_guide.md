# My Profile — User Guide

## What this page does

"My Profile" (the **Settings** item in the sidebar) shows your personal information and
lets you update your contact details — phone number, address, and personal email.

## Prerequisites

You must be signed in. Everything on the page is your own real account data.

## Using the page

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. The top card shows your avatar initials, name, email, job title, and role.
3. The **Personal details** card (left) shows information managed by HR:
   Name, Email, Job title, Department, and Hire date. These are read-only — the note at
   the bottom explains that only HR can change them.
4. The **Editable fields** card (right) is where you can make changes:
   - **Phone number** — your contact phone.
   - **Address** — your address.
   - **Personal email** — optional secondary email.
5. After editing, click **Save changes**. The button stays greyed out until you change
   something. A green confirmation appears when the save succeeds.
6. Click **Cancel** to undo your edits and restore the last saved values.
7. At the bottom of the right card, the **Password** section has a **Change password**
   button that takes you to the password-change screen.

## Flow

```
[Settings] → [View details] → [Edit contact fields] → [Save changes] → [Saved ✓]
                                    ↓
                              [Cancel] → restores previous values
                                    ↓
                        [Change password] → password screen
```

## Common mistakes

- **Save changes is greyed out** — you haven't changed anything yet.
- **Trying to edit Name / Job title / Department** — these are managed by HR; ask your
  HR team to update them.
- **Expecting a photo upload** — profile photos aren't supported; your initials are
  shown instead.

## FAQ

- **Why can't I edit my job title or department?** Those fields are controlled by HR for
  accuracy; the page only displays them.
- **Does Cancel call the server?** No — it simply restores the values as they were last
  saved.
- **Where do I change my password?** Use the **Change password** button in the Password
  section at the bottom of the Editable fields card.
- **Is the page available in Arabic?** Yes — the whole page, including RTL layout, works
  in both English and Arabic, and in light and dark mode.
